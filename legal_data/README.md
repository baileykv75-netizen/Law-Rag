# Legal Data

This directory is reserved for public, redistributable legal-source manifests, metadata, and later normalized legal corpus assets that are explicitly approved for inclusion in the repository.

The future legal knowledge layer must preserve legal identity and version metadata rather than storing anonymous text chunks only.

Expected metadata includes:

- authority/source ID;
- title;
- authority type/level;
- article number;
- article text;
- issuing body;
- publication/effective dates;
- repeal/expiry status where applicable;
- jurisdiction/scope;
- source/version information.

Private user contracts do not belong here.
